The road to election day

From "should I run?" to election night

Every candidate walks the same road. Here is each stop, what it asks of you, and where I come in.

  1. 1

    Deciding to run

    You care about something enough to put your name on a ballot. You are not sure you can win, or what it will cost you.

    Where I come in

    An honest read on the race: whether it is winnable, and what it takes in time, money and family.

  2. 2

    Choosing your lane

    A party, or your own platform. Which one fits what you believe, and which one gives you a real path to the seat.

    Where I come in

    We weigh the options together, then I introduce you to the organizers and local associations you need to know.

  3. 3

    Winning the nomination

    Before the voters choose you, the members do. That means signing people up and getting them into the room on the night.

    Where I come in

    The membership drive, the count, and a turnout plan for nomination night. Running as an independent? We go straight to step 4.

  4. 4

    Building your team

    A campaign manager, someone to handle the money, volunteers, and a reason for people to vote for you that fits in one breath.

    Where I come in

    I help you find the people, write the plan and the budget, and shape your story so it sounds like you.

  5. 5

    The campaign

    Doors, phones, signs, debates, reporters. Weeks of it, and every day there is more to do than time to do it.

    Where I come in

    I decide with you which doors and which voters come first, track who is with you, and get you ready for debates and interviews.

  6. 6

    Election day

    Everything comes down to whether the people who said yes at the door actually vote.

    Where I come in

    I run the get-out-the-vote operation from the advance polls until the last one closes.
